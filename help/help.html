❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I add a resident?

Go to the Residents module and click Add Resident.

How do I record maintenance payments?

Open the Maintenance module and enter the payment details.

How can I register a visitor?

Open the Visitors module and complete the visitor entry form.

How do I generate reports?

Navigate to the Reports module and choose the required report.
